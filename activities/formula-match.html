Naming clues that help

Elements that come in pairs: hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen and chlorine are written H2, O2, N2 and Cl2 when they are on their own.

Ending in -ide usually means just two elements: sodium chloride is NaCl, magnesium oxide is MgO.

Ending in -ate means oxygen is included: carbonate is CO3, sulfate is SO4, nitrate is NO3.

Prefixes count atoms: monoxide means one oxygen and dioxide means two, so carbon monoxide is CO and carbon dioxide is CO2.
