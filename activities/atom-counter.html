Atom counter

You can't balance an equation if you can't count atoms. Practise until it feels automatic.

Three rules for counting

H2SO4

Subscripts count the atom just before them. No subscript means 1.

H = 2, S = 1, O = 4

3H2O

Coefficients multiply every atom in the formula.

H = 3 × 2 = 6, O = 3 × 1 = 3

Ca(OH)2

A number after a bracket multiplies everything inside the bracket.

Ca = 1, O = 1 × 2 = 2, H = 1 × 2 = 2
