Part B: reaction patterns

Many reactions follow a pattern. Once you know the pattern, you can predict the products without being told.

metal + acid → salt + hydrogen

zinc + hydrochloric acid → zinc chloride + hydrogen

acid + metal hydroxide → salt + water

hydrochloric acid + sodium hydroxide → sodium chloride + water

acid + metal carbonate → salt + water + carbon dioxide

hydrochloric acid + calcium carbonate → calcium chloride + water + carbon dioxide

fuel + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water

methane + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water, when a fuel made of carbon and hydrogen burns completely

Naming the salt
